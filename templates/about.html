なぜこのプロジェクトを作ったのか

日本のテック業界で活躍したい方々に、より多くのチャンスと情報を届けたいという思いから、この求人ポータルを開発しました。大手サイトでは見つけにくい求人や、最新の働き方に関する案件もできるだけ幅広く掲載できるよう心がけています。
また、スクレイピング・データ処理・Web開発の技術力を総合的にアピールできる場としても本プロジェクトを位置付けています。

開発・仕事へのこだわり

  • データドリブン：実際の求人データを収集・分析し、ユーザーに価値ある情報を提供
  • ユーザー中心：シンプルで高速、スマホでも快適なUI/UXを追求
  • 自動化・効率化：Pythonによるスクレイピング・データ整形・API設計を徹底
  • 透明性・再現性：コード・データ処理の流れをドキュメント化し、誰でも検証可能に
  • 最新技術志向：FastAPI, MongoDB, Jinja2, Bootstrapなどモダンな技術を積極的に採用

My Story

While job hunting, I realized how exhausting it is to jump between multiple job boards just to find the right opportunities. I wished there was a single place to search jobs across all platforms.
During this New Year holiday, I decided to take on the challenge and build my own ideal job board from scratch.
Fueled by both technical curiosity and the desire to help others facing the same pain, I rapidly prototyped and launched this site.
"If you want something, build it yourself."—That spirit drives this project forward.

Why did I build this?

I created this job portal to empower people seeking tech careers in Japan with more opportunities and information. My goal is to make it easy to search and compare high-quality jobs, including those not listed on major sites, and to showcase my skills in scraping, data processing, and web development.

My Working Approach

  • Data-driven: Collecting and analyzing real job data to deliver value to users
  • User-focused: Simple, fast, and mobile-friendly UI/UX
  • Automation: Python-based scraping, data cleaning, and API design
  • Transparency: Documented code and data processing for reproducibility
  • Modern Tech: FastAPI, MongoDB, Jinja2, Bootstrap, and more

Highlights

  • Full ownership from requirements to deployment
  • Full-stack: data collection, cleaning, API, and UI
  • Performance, maintainability, and scalability in mind
  • UI design with Japanese/English support
  • Version control and CI/CD with Git/GitHub
